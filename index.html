Live API-sourced content

🌱

Symptom-based triage

The flowchart routes you from "what's happening" to the exact module you need in two clicks — a real decision tool, not decoration.

🔍

Search, sort & filter

Refine any topic by keyword, and sort results by duration or popularity — genuine interaction with live data.

📈

Progress tracking

Completion is saved server-side against an anonymous browser ID — no account, no email, no login screen.

🛡️

Graceful degradation

If the YouTube API is down, rate-limited, or unkeyed, the app automatically falls back to a curated verified video per topic — it never just breaks.

Load-balanced deployment

Runs identically on two web servers behind a round-robin load balancer, so no single server is a point of failure.

Before you dig in

Is this a real course?

No — Dr. NATH is a curated starting point, not a certification. Think of it as the difference between knowing how to swim and being a lifeguard. If you want certification, look into a local Red Cross or AHA course.

Where does the content come from?

Every video is pulled live from YouTube, filtered to channels run by recognized medical organizations. Nothing here is uploaded by us.

What if I'm in an emergency right now?

Stop reading and call your local emergency number immediately. This app is for learning ahead of time, not for real-time guidance during a crisis.
